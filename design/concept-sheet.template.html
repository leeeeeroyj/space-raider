SPACE RAIDER

Twin-stick dungeon raid. Fly a 6×6 vault of rooms, blast what guards the loot, and haul it to the extraction pad before your ship goes down.

Game screen

Live mockup at the game's real resolution (416×228, 16 px tiles). Click the screen, then fly it. Every sprite you pick below swaps in here.

Keyboard WASD move Mouse aim Hold click fire
Controller L stick move R stick aim and fire RT fire No controller yet. Press any button on it.
Aim Free aim. The beam leaves the hull edge facing your aim.
Top barScore, ships left, vault number and the value of the cargo you're carrying.
Room20×12 tiles. Doors differ in width per wall; some walls have none.
Vault map6×6 automap. Amber is you, cyan is the extraction room, red X is your wreck.
CargoTreasure you hold. Lose a ship and it drops at the X in that room.

Player ship

A round hull that never rotates. The beam leaves from whichever side or corner faces your aim, so pick a symmetric sprite that reads the same from every direction.

Hostiles

Six roles. Points are per kill except the Scavenger, which can't be destroyed: shooting it only drives it off.

Treasure

Five tiers. Each doubles the last. Bars are drawn to scale against the 8,000-point top tier.

Rooms & markers

Pixel Search sprites are objects, not seamless tiles. Walls repeat a single plate; the floor is drawn in code.

How a vault plays

36 roomsEach vault is a 6×6 grid. You enter at one room; one other room holds the extraction pad.
Carry, then bankTreasure only scores when you reach extraction. The more you carry out, the bigger the bonus.
Wreck markerLose a ship and your cargo drops where you died, marked X for one life. Dropped loot stays in that room until you pick it up or the game ends.
+10,000Bonus for entering every room in the vault before you extract.
Extra shipEvery 10,000 points.
